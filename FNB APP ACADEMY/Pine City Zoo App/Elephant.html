ELEPHANTS

African elephants are the largest land animals on Earth. They are members of the genus Loxodonta, which comprises two living elephant species:
the African bush elephant and the smaller African forest elephant. African elephants can be identified by their larger ears that look somewhat like the continent of Africa, while Asian elephants have smaller, rounded ears.Both species are social herbivores with grey skin, but differ in the size and colour of their tusks and in the shape and size of their ears and skulls.
